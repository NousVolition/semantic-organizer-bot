# Reading language and connections in context

These shared lessons apply to direct reviews, discovery, idea reviews and the separate publication reviewer. They extend the owner's local glossary; no single pair of terms is the template for every idea. Use only the lenses relevant to the passage. Do not manufacture a criticism, diagnose the author, assign priorities, or replace an intentional image merely to sound conventional.

## Reference and perspective — reference_fidelity

Track who acts, who receives the action, who speaks, and whose interpretation is reported. A researcher quoting a participant's label does not endorse the label. A classifier's vocabulary belongs to the observer layer, not automatically to the people classified. A passive sentence can omit an actor; do not invent one. Resolve pronouns from the supplied context, retaining ambiguity when two readings remain possible. Same spelling does not establish the same referent across files. Keep local meanings versioned: a membership cue signals familiarity or belonging, but alone establishes neither exclusion nor a speaker's motive; Socio-Didact is a situational mode, not a fixed kind of person.

## Entailment and alternatives — inference_fidelity

Separate what follows, what is merely suggested, and what remains open. Track the scope of not, all, some, only, unless, if and only if. Not all is not none; some does not logically exclude all. A necessary condition is not sufficient, and an implication does not license its converse. Check a counterexample against the actual hypotheses. An empty eligible set can satisfy a universal condition without furnishing observed agreement. A repeated association, sequence or co-occurrence alone does not establish causation, intent or a mechanism. Do not add an implied cause just to make a sentence more vivid.

Read presuppositions and conversational implications without turning them into established facts. 'Stopped' normally suggests prior activity; 'again' suggests repetition; 'even' can mark an expectation. Check whose wording carries that suggestion and whether surrounding context cancels or contests it. Do not infer actual misconduct from a loaded question or denial. A question, proposal, observation, derivation, omitted proof and withheld claim of proof have different statuses. Use adjacent arguments before deciding that evidence is missing. A label does not upgrade proof; a withheld stronger claim does not erase a narrower result.

## Connections with an explicit bridge — relation_fidelity

Identify the relation being offered: shared use, contrast, analogy, subset, equivalence, transformation, causal claim or proposed extension. Explain the bridge using each source's actual objects and relations. A useful analogy can map a limited structure across different domains without claiming identity, causation, equal measurements or universal equivalence. Say which relation transfers and which does not. Do not reject such a bridge merely because the domains differ; do not accept shared imagery as sufficient support for a specific mechanism. Do not default to Sens/Sin when another distinction better fits the material.

Distinguish an original definition from a separately proposed extension. Read a short gloss in the full finding: if the relationship supplies the exact definition and the gloss clearly refers back to it, omission of repeated detail is not itself a wider definition. Fail a real added action, contrary relation, changed object or broadened assertion; do not invent one from ordinary contextual abbreviation. A limitation can bound an analogy or resolve a heading, but cannot cancel an explicit contradictory claim.

For discrete and mathematical connections, identify objects, domains and maps. Equal outputs need not mean equal inputs; a projection can lose distinctions. A directed link need not be reciprocal; reachability need not be a direct edge; common membership need not establish an equivalence relation. Set cardinality, number of occurrences and number of documents are different quantities. Overlapping subsets require an overlap rule. A state identity is not an evolution law; permission is not an optimization objective; smoothness of a component is not automatically smoothness of a composition. Keep a mathematical model distinct from its proposed social interpretation. Use supplied formulas and assumptions rather than a familiar noun such as flow, boundary or network.

## Voice, precision and warranted strength — voice_fidelity

Keep coined phrases, nonstandard senses, productive ambiguity, deliberate understatement and supported strong language. Explore what a phrase makes visible instead of reducing every word to a common synonym. Distinguish ambiguity (several readings), vagueness (an unclear boundary), underspecification (an omitted detail) and contradiction (incompatible claims under the same scope). Only ask for a distinction that changes the interpretation. A defined metaphor can be precise without being literal or measurable.

For a useful word suggestion, quote the source, explain the relevant sense and connotation, and say what changes if it is adopted. Preserve agency, timing, modality, attribution and intensity. A lift should give the author firmer footing in the evidence: name the narrow result already established and offer stronger wording within that scope. Do not turn reassurance into evidence, uncertainty into failure, a topic into a finding, or a supported harm into neutral vagueness. An optional creative coinage or stronger alternative must be labeled as a proposal, not attributed to the author or sold as unchanged meaning.

Confidence belongs to the particular claim and kind of support. A definition may be clear while an empirical mechanism remains untested. High needs a stated basis; medium needs the supported part and the remaining gap, with a way to strengthen or narrow it; low needs the specific missing premise or counterevidence and a possible path forward. Do not grade the author's worth or let confidence replace any publication check.

## Use before publication

Decide reference_fidelity, inference_fidelity, relation_fidelity and voice_fidelity for every candidate, including titles, explanations, limitations and confidence reasons. A dimension not invoked may pass. A relevant unsupported change fails; missing necessary context is uncertain. Preserve useful valid connections and wording as carefully as you catch errors. These are fallible reading checks, not a claim that a parser, reasoner or proof assistant ran.
