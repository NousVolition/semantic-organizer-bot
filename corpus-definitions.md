# Owner's corpus definitions and mathematical distinctions

These are local definitions authorized by the owner for the combined corpus. Apply them when the source uses this framework; do not impose them on unrelated writing or silently override an explicit definition in another version. Identify a version conflict and retain both scopes. These notes guide generation and the independent comparison review. They do not certify a source's mathematics or run a solver.

## Preserve the language and its meaning

- **Sens**: personal intent or intended meaning in this corpus.
- **Sin**: socially constrained or encoded expression here, not moral wrongdoing. Sens and Sin are distinct stages or aspects in the author's model, not universal linguistic categories.
- **Socio-Didact**: a coined, situational mode of learning unwritten social rules through observation and experience, then deciding how much to adapt while preserving meaning. It does not imply a diagnosis, fixed cognitive type, superior intelligence, covert operation, control, or mastery. Quiet or peripheral participation is compatible with it.
- **Boundary margin**: modeled closeness to a change in local response. It requires an explicit representation and metric before numerical interpretation; it is not an exact measurement of a person or group.
- **Shibboleth / membership cue**: language, style, or ritual signaling familiarity or belonging. A cue alone does not establish manipulation, punishment, or exclusion. The author's “Shibboleth Check” is a locally named consistency audit, not a standard theorem.
- **Observer layer**: the researcher or classifier's vocabulary can itself be analyzed. Keep participant speech, researchers' descriptions, the report author's interpretation, and prior assistant claims attributed to their respective speakers.
- **Shared-Norm Orientation** and **Divergent Epistemological Orientation**: proposed situational priorities, not diagnoses, moral ranks, fixed neurotypes, or necessarily mutually exclusive classes. Coordination and independent verification may coexist. Do not convert the taxonomy's proposed tendencies into measured population differences.

Preserve unusual words, imagery, productive ambiguity, and supported semantic force. “Removed” and “responded to,” or “evaded” and “adapted,” do not necessarily mean the same thing. Suggest a conventional or softer word only when it preserves the intended distinction; explain any change of force. Do not treat a coinage as a factual error merely because it is unfamiliar.

## Check definitions before conclusions

1. **Alignment versus stability.** Four nodes with three coordinates each form a 4-by-3 position array; their Y projection has four components. A zero coordinate residual r_Y establishes alignment at that state. It is not a gradient, a feedback law, or proof of dynamic stability. A separately supplied evolution law and stability argument can support a stronger claim; do not reject such evidence just because the snapshot alone is insufficient.
2. **Consistency, context, and fairness.** In the binary audit W(i, action; c) is 1 for restriction and 0 for permission. Equality compares the same action in a declared relevant context c. The four pairs are 11, 10, 01, 00; equality holds for 11 and 00. Equal decisions alone do not establish equal reasons, equal restriction severity, or fairness. Negating a universal equality requires some unequal pair, not all pairs unequal. Within-context consistency may coexist with variation across contexts. Context must not be chosen merely to hide the actor difference being audited; demand an explicit relevance rule when needed. If W is instead a scalar score, retain that stated definition and do not apply the binary table silently.
3. **Boundary feasibility and sign.** Unsigned distance is never negative and does not distinguish the permitted side. A signed margin requires a declared permitted region, boundary, representation, and metric. No permitted available action means A intersect Omega is empty, not merely A intersect boundary(Omega) is empty. Distance to a nonempty boundary may still exist when passage is unavailable. Exact-edge margin m = epsilon and minimum clearance m >= epsilon are different constraints. A feasible point or maximizing action need not exist. Preserve an explicitly chosen exact-edge model instead of silently replacing it with minimum clearance.
4. **Reciprocal scaling.** diag(lambda, 1/lambda) requires lambda nonzero (positive if the model preserves signs). The scalar product SR is invariant under that paired transformation; conservation through time or equilibrium needs a separate dynamic argument. Do not reject the bounded algebraic identity itself.
5. **Threshold branches.** Let q = |grad S| - Gamma. The sharp response a0(q) = max(q, 0) is exactly zero for q <= 0 and equal to q for q > 0; it is continuous but has a corner at zero. An above-threshold formula alone does not specify the other branch. Zero added forcing does not imply the remaining dynamics are stable.
6. **The tanh tail and direction.** For epsilon > 0, at(q) = q*(1+tanh(q/epsilon))/2 is smooth in scalar q. For finite q < 0 its exact value is negative and nonzero (floating-point tanh can saturate). At q = -epsilon it is approximately -0.119203*epsilon. For f = -(sigma/rho)*at(q)*e_k with sigma > 0 and rho > 0, that tail reverses direction. Do not describe it as exactly off or as a nonnegative activation. An explicitly intended signed tail is a valid model choice, not automatically a mistake.
7. **Composition and zero gradients.** The Euclidean norm |v| is not differentiable at v = 0. A smooth outer tanh does not automatically make its composition smooth there; special cancellations must be assessed. One alternative is g_delta(v) = sqrt(v dot v + delta^2) - delta, delta > 0. It is smooth in v and zero at v = 0. Using q_delta = g_delta(grad S) - Gamma changes the measure near zero; for Gamma >= 0 its positive onset in the original magnitude is |grad S| > sqrt(Gamma^2 + 2*delta*Gamma). Do not hide that calibration change.
8. **Optional smooth exact-off law.** a_epsilon(q) = 0 for q <= 0, and q*exp[-(epsilon/q)^2] for q > 0, epsilon > 0, is C-infinity in scalar q with all derivatives matching at the join. Combined with q_delta and fixed delta > 0 it gives a smooth constitutive response in the gradient variable, exactly off below its defined threshold. It is an alternative, not a property of tanh. This local construction does not prove existence, uniqueness, or global smoothness of PDE solutions. Keep initial-field identities, constitutive regularity, and time-evolution claims distinct. A passage explicitly withholding a proof is not asserting that proof or claiming its conclusion is false.
9. **Force, stress, and units.** A fixed direction e_k does not guarantee drag: dissipation requires u dot f <= 0. For the stated incompressible Newtonian analogy, symmetric viscous stress is mu*(grad u + transpose(grad u)); mu*grad u alone is a scaled gradient tensor. A calibration Gamma = tau0/eta requires compatible units and eta > 0. At fixed tau0, increasing eta lowers Gamma; naming eta rigidity does not establish its proposed effect. Check domain, coupling, initial/boundary data and coefficient units before a stronger PDE claim.
10. **Memory, distributions, and scenario claims.** An accumulated input used as a source rate is integrated again by the evolution equation; it may be intentional memory. Distinguish the accumulator and its rate conversion from instantaneous production. A Dirac point source is a distribution, not a smooth function; a normalized smooth kernel is a different modeling choice. Advection u dot grad S equals conservative transport div(Su) only when div u = 0. A synthetic table must define its scoring and decision rule: with severity 5, modifiers .8, 1.4, 1.3 and thresholds 8.5, 5.5, 6, effective scores are 4, 7, 6.5; using score >= threshold restricts only the last two. Alignment scores do not derive those modifiers without a stated mapping. No feedback trajectory follows without an update rule. A reported count summing to 83 is not independent reproduction of the manuscript scan or proof of bias; retain version, matching and attribution limits.
